# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
Web, responsive across phones and desktop.

## Users
Davao residents looking for businesses nearby, merchants who manage their listings and requests, and studio staff who review leads and business records.

## Product Purpose
Make Davao businesses easier to find and give owners an online storefront. The studio also uses kanto62 to show its tools and book demos.

## Capabilities and Constraints
The directory supports search, filters, list and map views. Merchant flows include ownership review, editing, publishing, booking or stock requests, and plans and custom domains. Staff records and product recommendations stay in the restricted studio workspace. Keep public page content and links available in server-rendered HTML. Preserve documented pilot evidence and its scope.

## Brand Commitments
The product is kanto62. PixelPop and PixelCore are user-supplied references. Use official shadcn/ui components and lighter typography.
